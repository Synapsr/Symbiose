Privacy

Symbiose follows your meals, your activity and your health. That is personal data: the app is designed so it stays with you.

Updated November 6, 2026

In short

  • On your phone, by default. Your meals, photos, goals and notes are stored on your phone. We don’t receive them: no Symbiose account, no cloud.
  • The AI you choose. To analyse a meal or answer in the coach, Symbiose sends what is needed to the AI you picked (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, through OpenRouter, or an AI you run yourself).
  • No ads, no tracking. No ad network, no analytics, no advertising ID. Your data is never sold.
  • Open source. Symbiose’s code is public: anyone can check what this page says, on GitHub.

Who publishes Symbiose

Symbiose is a free, open-source app published by Synapsr. For any question about this page or your data: contact@synapsr.io.

Two ways to use Symbiose

On your phone (the default)

Everything is stored on your phone, inside the app. Synapsr receives none of it. You can make a backup (a file you keep wherever you like: iCloud Drive, Google Drive, your computer…) and restore it later.

With a Symbiose server

If you choose “I have a Symbiose server”, your data is stored on that server rather than on your phone alone.

  • A server you or someone you know runs: its owner is responsible for it. Synapsr has no access to it.
  • Synapsr’s server (by invitation): your data is kept there while your account exists, only to make the app work. No one but you can see it; the administrator only sees usage totals, never your meals or your health.

What the AI receives

When you photograph or describe a meal, or talk to the coach, Symbiose sends the AI you chose what it needs to answer: the meal’s photo or text, your goal and today’s targets, a few profile details (age, sex, height, weight) and, if you turned them on, parts of your day (activity, sleep, mood). Nothing more.

  • On your phone, these requests go straight from your phone to that provider, with your own account or key. They are then subject to that provider’s terms and privacy policy (for example OpenAI for ChatGPT).
  • Your key or sign-in is kept in the phone’s secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android) and only ever sent to that provider.
  • Without an AI, Symbiose still works: barcodes, manual entry, a plan built from keywords.

Health and sensors

  • Apple Health and Health Connect: only with your permission, Symbiose reads your steps, active energy, workouts, weight and height and, if you turn the matching modules on, your sleep, hydration, blood pressure and heart rate variability. It can save your meals, water and blood pressure there. This data is only used by the app, never for advertising, and is never sold.
  • Camera: to photograph a meal or scan a barcode.
  • Microphone: for dictation. On iPhone, speech recognition runs on the phone when it can; on Android, it goes through the phone’s speech recognition service. Symbiose only receives the text.
  • Notifications: for the reminders you turn on (morning check-in, sugar craving, streak).

Other services

When you scan a barcode, its number is sent to Open Food Facts to find the product. No other service is contacted, apart from the AI you chose and, if you use one, your Symbiose server.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • On your phone: Me → Your data → “Erase all my data” erases everything Symbiose keeps. Deleting the app does the same.
  • On a server: Me → “Delete my account” deletes your account and all its data, right away.
  • On Synapsr’s server, you can also ask by email at contact@synapsr.io: we do it within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export (backup) or delete your data from the app. For Synapsr’s server, you can exercise these rights by email. You can also contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Age and health

Symbiose is meant for adults (18 and over). It is not a medical device: it makes no diagnosis and does not replace advice from a health professional.
